Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in the order to play them. Stage 1 is the story; the rest stays open after the credits. Around 200 hours in total, long rather than hard — no difficulty trophies, no online trophies, no mission medals.

Missables & at-risk trophies

One trophy can truly be missed. One more is at risk if you leave a time-sensitive strand too late. Everything else stays available after the credits.

Save discipline — the habit that protects the whole run

The game has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, and it gives you 15 manual save slots plus one autosave. Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter and rotate the rest. Keep one untouched save from just before the final mission. Keep your Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops. Never use cheats on your trophy save — cheats are expected to switch off trophies and saving.

Sources & honesty

Every guide in this ledger is written in our own words, and every trophy's guide names where its facts come from. The official trophy names and descriptions are shown as PlayStation publishes them — that is the one exception to the own-words rule.

Where the facts come from

  • Exophase and PSNProfiles — the official trophy names, descriptions, grades, hidden flags and the counts PlayStation tracks. Both lists agree on all 55, checked live on 19 Nov 2026.
  • OutputLag, GTA 6 trophy list, 19 Nov 2026 — launch-week methods: the balcony meditation, the Yago race, the Snap Gun, the vaults, the jet at gates E3 and E4, the tank at Mile Zero, the 100% checklist contents, and the five trophies with no confirmed method.
  • Push Square, GTA 6 trophy guide & roadmap, 19 Nov 2026 — 397 stunts in the game in total, and the 100% checklist appearing after the story.
  • Planet GTA 6, GTA 6 trophies, 19 Nov 2026 — cross-check of the 27 visible and 28 hidden trophies.
  • GGRecon, GTA 6 trophy list, 19 Nov 2026 — GTABase: no online or DLC trophies, and nine activity types for Master of None.

What we do not know yet

  • Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch: In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their guides say so plainly instead of inventing a method.
  • Personal Best is reported to pop when Jason and Lucia both reach maximum Strength — reported, not confirmed.
  • Whether Positive Mental Attitude strictly needs the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is not confirmed — do it anyway, and keep a Chapter 1 save.
  • Launch-day methods are marked unconfirmed wherever the sources mark them. Where a method is not pinned down, the guide says so.
