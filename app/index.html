Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions alone: play the story first, then the side stories, then crime and activities, then money and the odd moments, and finish with the 100% checklist. Only one trophy can truly be missed — see Missables. Everything else stays available after the credits, so there is no need to rush the free-roam grind while the story is still running.

Platinum time, as reported at launch: around 200 hours, most of it the 100% checklist. There are no difficulty trophies, no online trophies and no mission medals: the platinum is long, not hard. Keep cheats off your trophy save — cheats are reported to switch off trophies and saving.

Missable trophies

Built from the official descriptions and the cited launch-day guide. The short version: keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one from just before the final mission — there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus at launch.

Your save-slot plan

  1. Save at the start of every chapter (the game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave — rotate the rest).
  2. In Chapter 1, do Lucia’s balcony meditation before the chapter ends, and keep that Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops.
  3. In Chapter 3, race Yago the moment his race appears (hourglass icon) — save first, then race.
  4. Keep one untouched manual save from just before the final mission.
  5. Never test cheats on your trophy save — use a throwaway save if you try them.

Sources & honesty

GTA VI launched on 19 Nov 2026, the day this guide was written. Every trophy’s name, description and grade is shown exactly as PlayStation publishes it. The launch-day methods are cited per trophy; where no source pins a place or method down, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one.

  • Official names, descriptions, grades and PSN counts: Exophase — Grand Theft Auto VI Trophies (PS5), checked 19 Nov 2026; PSNProfiles — Grand Theft Auto VI Trophies, checked 19 Nov 2026. The two agreed on every name, description and grade: 55/55, 0 mismatches.
  • Launch-day methods, chapter timing, missable warning and 100% checklist: OutputLag — GTA 6 Trophy List: All 55 Trophies and Achievements, 19 Nov 2026. Methods it marks unconfirmed are marked unconfirmed here too.
  • Cross-checks on totals and the single missable: GamesRadar+, Press Start and PC Gamer (same 55, same grades); GTABase and PowerPyx (Positive Mental Attitude flagged as the only possibly missable trophy).
  • What is not confirmed yet: five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method at launch — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin. Their guides say so, and give the description as the only hint. Two more methods are reported but not confirmed: Personal Best (both characters at maximum Strength) and the strict-need question for the Chapter 1 balcony meditation.
