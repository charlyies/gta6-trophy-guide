Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in the order to play them. Stage 1 is the story; the rest stays open after the credits. Around 200 hours in total, long rather than hard — no difficulty trophies, no online trophies, no mission medals.

Missables & at-risk trophies

One trophy can truly be missed. One more is at risk if you leave a time-sensitive strand too late. Everything else stays available after the credits.

Sources & honesty

Every guide on this page is written in our own words. The official trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown as PlayStation publishes them, and every guide names the source its facts come from.

Where the list comes from

Names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags: the official PlayStation 5 list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles, cross-checked live on 19 November 2026 via Planet GTA 6 and OutputLag. The two trackers agree on every name, description and grade: 55 trophies, 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden, 0 online.

Where the methods come from

Launch-day methods are cited per trophy from the OutputLag GTA 6 trophy list of 19 November 2026, with cross-checks from Press Start (18 November 2026) and GGRecon (19 November 2026). Where OutputLag marks a method unconfirmed, this guide says so in plain words and does not pretend otherwise.

What is not pinned down yet

Six odd-moment trophies — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — have no confirmed unlock method at launch. Their guides say so honestly and give the best reading of the official description instead of inventing a method. Personal Best should pop when Jason and Lucia both reach maximum Strength, and the Chapter 1 balcony meditation counting toward Positive Mental Attitude is not confirmed either; both are marked as such.

Save discipline

There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus at launch. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one untouched save from just before the final mission. The game has 15 manual save slots plus one autosave.

Cheats

PowerPyx reports that turning on a cheat disables trophies, and GameSpot and PC Gamer add that you cannot save once you have used one. Never use cheats on your trophy save.
